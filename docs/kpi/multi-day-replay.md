Generated by `python -m raildss_orchestrator multiday report --dir docs/kpi/multi-day` from the runs in `docs/kpi/multi-day/`. For each of 5 real days (2025-08-05, 2025-08-12, 2025-08-15, 2025-08-20, 2025-08-22) the decision loop runs over the window 6-12 h with the greedy planner (**BL-1**, controller practice) and with the CP-SAT rolling horizon (**BL-2**, the DSS), both with auto-accept. The day is then replayed in the twin under the orders each arm issued and compared with **BL-0** (no decision support) on the same day, demand (the real freight day) and seed. KPIs are the day's (FR-KPI-05); D1, D2, H1 and H2 are measured in the loop window. A difference is the arm minus BL-0 (or BL-2 minus BL-1); lower is better for A5, delays, stops, detention and alerts, higher for punctuality and the conflict KPIs.

- **BL-2 against BL-0:** the weighted delay (A5) falls on 4 of 5 days (mean -245.8 train-min, standard deviation 307.5, range -675.7 to +44.1). It rises on 2025-08-12 (+44.1). (A low rank means little delay to remove: 2025-08-12 has BL-0 A5 6933, number 1 of 5 from the lowest.)
- **BL-2 against BL-1:** A5 is lower with BL-2 on 2 of 5 days (mean +125.8 train-min, range -122.5 to +763.0), so BL-2 does not beat the greedy loop on A5 in this sample.
- Unplanned stops fall on every day with BL-1 and BL-2.
- Share of candidate plans that passed G0-G3: BL-1 100 %, BL-2 64 %. The loop releases only verified plans, so a low share means the gates rejected most of the candidates of that arm.
