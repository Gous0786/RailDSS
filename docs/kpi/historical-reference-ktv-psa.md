## Definitions

- **through_transit**: last minus first arrival at section stations, loads calling at >= min stations; a load belongs to the day it enters the section
- **dwell**: departure minus arrival at section stations; a call belongs to its arrival day
- **loads_per_day**: distinct loads per adjacent block section by arrival day; month figure is the mean over days with traffic on the block
- **passenger_delay**: not available: the log has no passenger actuals
